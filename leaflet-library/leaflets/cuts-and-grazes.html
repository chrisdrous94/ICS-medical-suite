Cuts and grazes

Your child had a cut or graze today. Keep the area clean and watch for signs of infection over the next few days.

Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

What we did

School staff can mark the care given on the printed leaflet.

  • Applied pressure to stop bleeding.
  • Rinsed the wound.
  • Covered the wound with a dressing.

Incident time and additional notes

Home care

  • Press firmly with a clean cloth or dressing.
  • If blood comes through, add another dressing on top and keep pressing.
  • If something is embedded, leave it in place and press on either side.
  • Once a small wound stops bleeding, rinse it with clean tap water.
  • Clean the skin around it with soap and water, then cover the wound with a sterile dressing.

Checking the wound over the next few days

  • Change the dressing if it becomes wet or dirty.
  • Get medical advice if pain, swelling or redness increases, pus develops or your child has a fever.
  • Ask the clinician whether a dirty wound needs a tetanus booster.
Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

Red flags

  • Call 112 if bleeding is severe and will not stop.
  • A very deep or large wound, loss of feeling or difficulty moving the area needs emergency assessment.
  • Seek prompt advice for a bite, a dirty wound or a wound that stays dirty after rinsing.
