Sprains and strains

Your child hurt a joint or muscle today. Pain and swelling can change over the next few hours. A bad sprain and a broken bone can look similar.

Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

What we did

School staff can mark the care given on the printed leaflet.

  • Stopped activity and rested the injured area.
  • Applied a wrapped cold pack.
  • Supported or raised the injured area.

Incident time and additional notes

Home care

  • For a mild injury, protect and rest it for the first two to three days.
  • Use a cold pack wrapped in a towel for up to 20 minutes every two to three hours, and raise the area if comfortable.

Getting back to activity

  • Avoid heat and massage in the first couple of days.
  • Once pain allows, encourage gentle movement so the joint does not stiffen.
  • Keep your child out of PE and sport while normal movement still hurts.
  • Get advice if the injury is failing to improve or your child still limps.
Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

Red flags

  • Seek urgent assessment if your child cannot bear weight or use the injured limb, or pain, bruising or swelling is worsening.
  • Go to an emergency department if the limb looks misshapen, is numb or feels cold.
