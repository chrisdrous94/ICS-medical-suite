Suspected poisoning

Your child may have been exposed to a harmful substance. Some symptoms develop later. Get emergency help immediately if exposure is suspected.

Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

What we did

School staff can mark the care given on the printed leaflet.

  • Called for emergency help.
  • Stayed with your child and watched their breathing.
  • Identified the substance or kept the packaging for responders.

Incident time and additional notes

Home care

  • Stay with your child and monitor their breathing.
  • Follow instructions from emergency responders.
  • Avoid making them vomit or giving them food or drink unless a medical professional directs you.

What to tell emergency responders

  • Keep the product packaging or medicine container if safe.
  • Tell responders what the substance was, the possible amount, when it happened and what symptoms you have noticed.
  • Bring the label or take a photo if you can do so without delaying care.

Red flags

  • If you think your child has swallowed, touched or breathed in something harmful, get emergency help immediately.
  • Call 112 especially if they are unusually drowsy, have breathing problems, have a seizure or lose consciousness.
  • Symptoms can take time to appear, so act even if your child seems well.
