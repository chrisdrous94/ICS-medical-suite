Head Injury & Bumps

Your child had a bump to the head today. Even after a minor bump, symptoms can develop later. Please keep a close eye on them for the next 24 hours.

Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

What we did

School staff can mark the care given on the printed leaflet.

  • Applied a wrapped cold compress.
  • Watched for symptoms after the bump.
  • Provided quiet rest time.

Incident time and additional notes

Home care

  • If home care is appropriate, have an adult stay with your child for at least 24 hours.
  • Let them rest and sleep if tired. Avoid strenuous activity. Ask their clinician when they can return to PE or contact sports.
  • For swelling, hold a cold pack wrapped in a towel against the bump for short periods.
  • For a mild headache, use children’s paracetamol if suitable for your child. Follow the directions on the pack.
Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

Red flags

Call 112 now if your child:

  • Is very drowsy, hard to wake or has a seizure.
  • Seems confused or acts unusually, or has trouble speaking, understanding you, seeing or walking.
  • Has blood coming from an ear or clear fluid from the nose or ears.

Call 112 after a high-speed impact or a fall over 1 metre or 5 stairs.

Get urgent medical advice if they vomit, feel dizzy or develop a headache that worsens or does not go away.
