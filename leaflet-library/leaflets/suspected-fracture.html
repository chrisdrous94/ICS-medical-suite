Suspected broken arm or wrist

Your child hurt their arm or wrist today. A break can look like a sprain and needs prompt medical assessment if suspected.

Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

What we did

School staff can mark the care given on the printed leaflet.

  • Stopped activity and supported the arm.
  • Applied a wrapped cold pack.
  • Arranged further medical assessment.

Incident time and additional notes

Home care

  • Support the arm where it is comfortable, for example with a towel as a sling.
  • Leave it in that position and avoid trying to straighten it.
  • Remove rings or a watch if they slide off easily before swelling increases.
  • Use a cold pack wrapped in a towel for up to 20 minutes at a time.
  • If there is a wound, cover it with a clean dressing.

Watching the fingers while waiting for care

  • Tell the clinician if the fingers change colour or sensation.
  • Follow the treating team’s instructions on cast care and return to school or PE.

Red flags

  • A suspected break needs prompt medical assessment.
  • Go to an emergency department if the arm or wrist looks out of shape, the hand is numb or tingling, or bone is visible.
  • Call 112 for severe bleeding or if your child cannot be moved safely.
