Asthma attack

Your child had breathing symptoms that may be related to asthma. Follow their asthma action plan and arrange follow-up after an attack.

Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

What we did

School staff can mark the care given on the printed leaflet.

  • Helped your child sit upright and stayed with them.
  • Helped them use their prescribed reliever.
  • Followed their asthma plan and arranged further help.

Incident time and additional notes

Home care

  • Help your child sit upright and stay with them.
  • Use the prescribed reliever as directed in their asthma action plan.
  • Use a spacer if that is part of their instructions.
  • Tell the ambulance service what medicine was given and when.

After an asthma attack

  • Arrange a medical review within two days after an attack, even if your child feels well.
  • Check that the school has the current asthma plan and knows where the inhaler and spacer are kept.
Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

Red flags

  • Call 112 if your child is struggling to breathe, gets worse at any point, has no reliever inhaler during the attack or does not improve after the maximum treatment in their asthma plan.
