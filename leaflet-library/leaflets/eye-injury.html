Eye injuries

Your child hurt or irritated an eye today. Please watch for ongoing pain or any change in how clearly they can see.

Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

What we did

School staff can mark the care given on the printed leaflet.

  • Helped your child stop rubbing their eye.
  • Rinsed the eye with clean water.
  • Arranged further assessment.

Incident time and additional notes

Home care

  • For rinsing, hold the eyelids open as best you can and use plenty of clean, gently flowing water for at least 20 minutes.
  • Take the chemical container or label if it is safe to bring.
  • For loose dust, rinse with clean water.
  • Avoid rubbing the eye and leave any embedded object in place.

Watching for changes in vision or pain

  • Seek advice if a minor injury has not improved within 24 hours, or sooner if you are worried about your child.
  • Avoid contact lenses until the eye has recovered.
Life-threatening emergency? Call an ambulance in Cyprus.
Call 112

Red flags

  • Get emergency care for a strong chemical splash, an embedded object, a high-speed impact, severe eye pain or any change in vision.
  • If a strong chemical gets into the eye, start rinsing immediately and continue while help is arranged.
